NexGen invests, builds, and operates distributed gas-to-digital Infrastructure. We convert wasted (flare), stranded, and excess energy production capacity from upstream producers into reliable, efficient, and sustainable energy — powering data centers, industrial zones, and emerging digital ecosystems across the Middle East.
